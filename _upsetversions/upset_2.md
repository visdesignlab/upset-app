A re-implementation of the original UpSet. Key new features are undo/redo functionality, embedding, and being able to download sets of items in a csv file. UpSet 2 is available on the web and includes data hosting. It can also be integrated into your web-application as a REACT component. 

UpSet 2 auto-generates text-descriptions (alt-text) for the plot, to make UpSet plots more accessible.
